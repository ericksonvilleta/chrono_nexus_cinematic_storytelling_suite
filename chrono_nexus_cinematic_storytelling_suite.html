About Chrono-Nexus

Chrono-Nexus Odyssey is a split-screen cinematic storytelling experience featuring 3D mouse parallax layers, interactive narrative acts, and synthesized Web Audio ambient soundscapes.

  • Split-Screen Layout: Narrative storytelling on the left, immersive 3D parallax viewport on the right.
  • Interactive Chapters: Switch between acts to dynamically update visuals, metrics, and soundscapes.
  • Web Audio Synthesizer: Generates real-time ambient chords and pulse tones.
